This Order may be signed concurrently with the master contracts or separately before Phase 1 final handover. Service Orders are signed concurrently with the two contracts and carry equal legal effect.

## 2. Scope of Services

Party B shall provide the following remote advisory services to Party A:

- Post-deployment workflow tuning (prompt, playbook, workflow adjustments)
- Connector planning and MCP expansion guidance
- Structured guidance for incremental knowledge base imports
- Phase 2 technical assessment and recommendations
- System-level remote troubleshooting (non-hardware)
- Other remote advisory work confirmed in writing by both parties

**Expressly excluded**: Hardware fault repair (covered under HWOS contract warranty), on-site support (separate On-Site Service Order), handover briefings and initial workflow demonstrations within fixed delivery scope.
