# Cubecloud On-Site Service Order

> This Service Order is a standalone agreement, referencing but not modifying the following master contracts:
> - Cubecloud Local AI Hardware Equipment and Standard System Delivery Contract (Contract No.: CUBECLOUD-HWOS-2026-P01)
> - Cubecloud Legal Agent Application Customization Service Contract (Contract No.: CUBECLOUD-LEGALAGENT-2026-P01)
>
> The services under this Order are not part of the fixed delivery scope of the above two contracts. This Order may be signed after the master contracts, upon confirmation of actual on-site service requirements.
>
> **Note**: The 3 business days of first-delivery on-site installation and training are already included in the two-contract fixed delivery at no extra charge and do not require this Order.

## 2. Scope of Services

Party B shall provide the following on-site support services to Party A:

- System upgrade and performance tuning (on-site)
- On-site workshops and advanced training
- On-site troubleshooting and issue reproduction
- New hardware / peripheral on-site integration and verification
- Other on-site work confirmed in writing by both parties

**Expressly excluded**: First-delivery 3 business days on-site installation and basic training (already in fixed delivery), hardware manufacturer warranty repairs, remote advisory services (separate Remote Advisory Service Order).

## 7. Cancellation and Rescheduling

| Item | Agreement |
|------|-----------|
| Party A cancellation | No charge with 5+ business days' written notice; within 5 business days, Party B's incurred travel costs shall be borne by Party A |

| Rescheduling | New service dates to be confirmed by both parties |
