# Cubecloud Annual License Renewal Order

> This Service Order is a standalone agreement, referencing but not modifying the following master contracts:
> - Cubecloud Local AI Hardware Equipment and Standard System Delivery Contract (Contract No.: CUBECLOUD-HWOS-2026-P01)
> - Cubecloud Legal Agent Application Customization Service Contract (Contract No.: CUBECLOUD-LEGALAGENT-2026-P01)
>
> The services under this Order are not part of the fixed delivery scope of the above two contracts. Year 1 licensing and support is included in fixed delivery; this Order covers annual renewal from Year 2 onwards.

## 2. Scope of Services

Party B shall provide the following annual licensing and software environment support to Party A:

- Cubecloud Standard AI Layer license renewal (2 units)
- Cubecloud Agent OS Surface license renewal (2 units)
- Software updates and version upgrades
- Basic operational support (remote)

**Expressly excluded**: Hardware fault repair (covered under HWOS contract warranty), on-site support (separate On-Site Service Order), remote advisory services (separate Remote Advisory Service Order), new feature custom development.

## 6. Validity and Termination

| Item | Agreement |
|------|-----------|
| Order validity | 12 months from the first renewal date; automatically extended for 12 months upon each annual renewal |
| Non-renewal | If Party A provides written notice of non-renewal 30 days before the renewal date, this Order terminates on expiry; software licensing and operational support cease upon termination |
| Early termination | Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice; paid but unexpired fees refunded on a pro-rata basis |
| Post-termination obligations | Confidentiality, data return, and dispute resolution provisions survive termination |
