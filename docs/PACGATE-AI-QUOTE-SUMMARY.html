Advisory & On-Site

Support services

  • All optional services, support, and add-on charges are listed in the consolidated Optional Services & Add-On Schedule.
  • 40-hour remote advisory reserve (1 year validity): CNY 20,000, via standalone Remote Advisory Service Order. Client has selected this option for the current contract.
  • Annual license renewal (Year 2 onwards): USD 611 / CNY 4,400 / year, via standalone Annual License Renewal Order (priced at 25% of initial license fee).
  • First-delivery on-site installation and training (3 business days): included in fixed delivery at no extra charge.
  • On-site support: 5-day package at CNY 43,200 reference price, via standalone On-Site Service Order (travel extra).
  • Travel and accommodation: billed separately at cost or quoted per trip.

Excluded or Deferred

  • The full Pacgate SaaS build is not included in this Phase 1 quotation.
  • End-client pricing, OEM licensing, and later platform commercial models are deferred until after the pilot produces evidence.
  • Travel, customer-owned research subscriptions, third-party data licences, and any separately applied CNY invoice VAT reserve are excluded from the fixed delivery subtotal.
  • Any scope expansion beyond the agreed pilot package is quoted separately.
