Cubecloud Local Legal AI Pilot · Revised May 2026

Pacgate AI - Phase 1 Pilot Plans

This revision replaces the old full-build SaaS quotation with a services-first plan: two local AI machines, two tailored legal-agent deployments, starter knowledge and RAG enablement, and long-term advisory support. The full Pacgate SaaS build remains a later decision, not the thing being purchased now.

What Pacgate is buying now

Cubecloud delivers a practical local pilot that Pacgate can operate, study, and expand: hardware first, legal agents second, knowledge structure third, and product discovery after real use. The current working commercial basis is USD 16,612 / CNY 119,600 for fixed delivery, with advisory held separate.

What is intentionally deferred

The full Pacgate SaaS quotation, broader end-client pricing, and later OEM or licensing economics are held back until Phase 1 proves the operating model and gives Pacgate real usage evidence.

Commercial Thesis

Pacgate does not need to buy the entire product vision on day one. The stronger commercial move is to buy a private pilot that produces immediate operating value: two local AI-capable machines under Cubecloud's standard layer, two legal-agent adaptations for side-by-side learning, and a starter knowledge foundation that begins compounding from the first engagement.

Current pricing assumes three fixed delivery packages totaling USD 16,612 / CNY 119,600, with advisory billed separately. USD figures are tax/VAT included; CNY figures on this page are shown at a reference FX rate of USD 1 = CNY 7.20 and exclude the additional 3.5% VAT reserve.
Immediate Value

Private AI for daily legal work

  • Keep sensitive work on Pacgate-controlled hardware.
  • Let the team compare two strong open-source legal system patterns side by side.
  • Start building a private legal knowledge asset immediately instead of waiting for a later platform.
Rollout Logic

Services first, platform later

  • Phase 1: deployment and professional services.
  • Phase 2: managed support or subscription only if the operating model proves out.
  • Later: Pacgate SaaS or broader OEM packaging after real workflow evidence exists.

OpenSpace for shared control, Open WebUI for private AI work, IronClaw for security boundaries, Hermes for memory and scheduled tasks, Warp ADE and OpenCode for tuning and extension work.

Knowledge Layer

Starter context graph and RAG

Future Pacgate SaaS blueprint

The Rust workspace in this repository remains the future-state product blueprint, but it is no longer presented as the immediate paid build scope.

Workstream 6

Advisory and Phase 2 discovery

Remote or on-site assistance, workflow tuning, connector planning, and later SaaS discovery only after Phase 1 produces enough evidence to justify that next quote.

Phase 2 Decision Gate

Only after the pilot succeeds should Pacgate and Cubecloud reopen the larger product question.

Decision 01

Which workflows are repeatable enough to productize?

Use real machine usage, lawyer feedback, and workflow logs instead of guessing too early.

Decision 02

Which legal-agent patterns should become proprietary Pacgate features?

Use side-by-side learning from Claude for Legal and Lavern to decide what Pacgate should own later.

Decision 03

What data model should a Pacgate SaaS platform actually use?

Base that answer on matter folders, authority structures, context-graph usage, and retrieval behavior observed in Phase 1.
