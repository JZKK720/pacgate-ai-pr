Assumptions

Commercial assumptions

  • Pricing assumes two AIPC-class systems with external GPU dock pairings sized for the Phase 1 pilot.
  • Adaptation fees assume targeted Pacgate tailoring, not a greenfield legal product build.
  • USD figures are tax/VAT included. CNY figures are shown at the proposal reference FX rate and do not yet include the additional 3.5% VAT reserve; invoice currency and FX/tax treatment are confirmed at contracting time.
  • Customer provides nominated reviewers, source materials, and timely sign-off during milestone demos and handover.
Exclusions

Outside fixed delivery scope

  • Travel, accommodation, customer-owned research subscriptions, and third-party data licences.
  • Any full Pacgate SaaS build, downstream client pricing, or OEM licensing structure.
  • Any connector, workflow, or deployment expansion outside the agreed Phase 1 package.
  • Any hardware changes beyond the confirmed procurement configuration.
Advisory & On-Site

Support services

  • All optional services, support, and add-on charges are listed in the consolidated Optional Services & Add-On Schedule.
  • 40-hour remote advisory reserve (1 year validity): CNY 20,000, via standalone Remote Advisory Service Order. Client has selected this option for the current contract.
  • Annual license renewal (Year 2 onwards): USD 611 / CNY 4,400 / year, via standalone Annual License Renewal Order (priced at 25% of initial license fee).
  • First-delivery on-site installation and training (3 business days): included in fixed delivery at no extra charge.
  • On-site support: 5-day package at CNY 43,200 reference price, via standalone On-Site Service Order (travel extra).
Change Control

Handling additions

  • Out-of-scope requests are handled by separate mini-SOW or advisory billing.
  • The fixed delivery subtotal applies only to the agreed Phase 1 package structure.
  • Any material FX refresh or procurement change should be documented before invoicing.
Drafting Note

Suggested use

  • 本报价基于两套适配第一阶段试点的 AIPC 级系统与外接 GPU Dock 组合。
  • 适配费用基于 Pacgate 定向 tailoring，而不是从零建设新法律产品。

排除项
