Annex D - Future Platform Carve-Out

Phase 2 Options Note

The client's platform questions are valid, but they belong to a different buying decision than the current pilot. This note keeps those questions organized while protecting the existing Phase 1 quote. The options below are planning directions only, not current commitments.

Budget, timeline, and architecture for any Phase 2 platform should only be fixed after Phase 1 produces real usage evidence and a separate discovery step is complete.

Indicative Option Tiers

Option tier Primary goal Likely additions beyond Phase 1 Commercial posture
Seed customer pilot edition Allow a narrow set of external users or matters to pass through a supervised prototype flow. External intake surfaces, stronger matter isolation, disclaimers, controlled release flow, support-ready audit views. Best treated as a limited extension or discovery-backed pilot quote.
MVP commercial edition Support early repeatable client delivery with clearer operations and permissions. Customer and lawyer roles, organization isolation, more robust storage and retrieval, admin tooling, observability, operational playbooks. Requires a separate product-stage statement of work and a stronger hosting decision.
Formal platform edition Operate as a true multi-customer product environment. Tenant management, commercial controls, billing and support systems, stronger security and compliance architecture, production operations. Should be treated as a separate platform program, not as a continuation of the original pilot quote.

What Phase 1 Can Reuse

Reusable assets
  • Prompt and workflow discipline
  • Scenario evidence from real lawyer use
  • Authority structure and private knowledge organization
  • Pilot deployment experience and runbook logic
Likely rework areas
  • Production hosting topology
  • Customer identity and tenancy model
  • Commercial operations and billing layer
  • Platform-grade observability, queueing, and service controls

When to Open Phase 2 Discovery

Trigger 1

Pacgate has evidence from actual Phase 1 operation

Future product design becomes much stronger once the team has real usage, review, and handover evidence instead of only theory.

Trigger 2

The desired customer entry model is known

The right Phase 2 architecture depends heavily on whether the next step is a seed-customer pilot, a lawyer-operated service, or a broader product model.

Trigger 3

Commercial and legal constraints are explicit

Open-source obligations, data-location expectations, tenancy design, and service levels all affect the real Phase 2 quote and should be known before numbers are fixed.

附件 D - 未来平台化 carve-out

第二阶段选项说明

客户关于平台化的问题是合理的，但它们属于不同于当前试点采购的另一类商业决策。本说明的作用，是在不破坏第一阶段报价边界的前提下，把这些问题继续整理清楚。以下内容只代表方向，不构成当前承诺。

第二阶段平台的预算、周期和架构，应在第一阶段产生真实使用证据后，再通过单独的 discovery 或 scoping 工作确认。

指向性选项层级

选项层级 主要目标 相较第一阶段可能新增的内容 商务处理方式
种子客户试点版 让少量外部用户或真实 matters 进入受控原型流程。 外部入口、加强的 matter 隔离、免责声明、受控释放流、面向支持的审计视图。 更适合作为有限扩展或基于 discovery 的试点报价。
MVP 商业版 支持更可重复的早期客户交付，并具备更清晰的运营和权限控制。 客户与律师角色、组织隔离、更稳健的存储与检索、后台工具、可观测性和运营手册。 需要单独的产品阶段工作说明以及更明确的部署决策。
正式平台版 形成真正面向多客户运行的产品环境。 租户管理、商业控制、计费与客服系统、更强的安全合规架构和生产运维体系。 应作为独立的平台化项目，而非原始试点报价的自然延续。

第一阶段可复用的部分

可复用资产
  • Prompt 与 workflow 纪律
  • 真实律师使用场景下积累的证据
  • Authority 结构与私有知识组织方式
  • 试点部署经验与运行手册逻辑
高概率需要重做的部分
  • 生产级部署拓扑
  • 客户身份体系与租户模型
  • 商业运营与计费层
  • 平台级可观测性、任务调度和服务控制

何时开启第二阶段 discovery

触发条件 1

Pacgate 已获得第一阶段真实运行证据

未来产品设计会明显依赖第一阶段积累的真实使用、复核和交付证据，而不只是纸面假设。

触发条件 2

目标客户入口模型已经明确

第二阶段架构会高度依赖“下一步是种子客户试点、律师代运营服务，还是更广的平台模式”这一前提。

触发条件 3

商业与法律边界已经明确

开源义务、数据位置要求、租户设计和服务等级都会影响真实第二阶段报价，需在数字落定前先确认清楚。
